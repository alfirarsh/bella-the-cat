ABOUT ME

Hi! My name is Bella, and I’m a four years old orange cat born on April 19, 2022. 🧡 I’m a pretty simple girl. My daily routine basically consists of grooming myself, sleeping, eating, and pooping. Sometimes I wake up around 4–5 AM just because I’m hungry and need to make sure my humans know about it. 😼 My favorite food is wet food with a fish flavor. I also have a very special friend, a fish plushie! 🐟 We may not be able to have conversations, but I like having my little fish friend around. I’m also a little bit moody. Sometimes I can be super clingy and want attention all day, but then suddenly I’ll act like I don’t even know you. 😭 I guess that’s just part of my charm. Oh, and I have a very special talent: slipping for absolutely no reason. Especially on the stairs. I don’t know why it happens, but apparently gravity really likes me. :) Overall, I’m just a sleepy, hungry, orange girl who enjoys doing absolutely nothing! And honestly, I’m pretty good at it. 🐈🧡.
